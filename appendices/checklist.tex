\chapter{Checklist Before Submission}
\label{apx:checklist}

Go through this list before you submit your thesis.
Call \texttt{\textbackslash hideinstructions} in \texttt{thesis.tex} to remove this checklist, and all other instructions, from the rendered PDF.

\begin{itemize}
\item[$\square$] Check that references are correctly formatted.
\item[$\square$] Do not insert blank pages between chapters, the table of contents, and similar.
  (If you intend to print your thesis this can be useful, but most people read it on screen, and it is annoying to scroll over empty pages.)
\item[$\square$] Use the \verb|~| symbol to keep references together with a specific word, e.g., \verb|Paxos~\cite{paxos}|.
\item[$\square$] Use the \texttt{cleveref} package and the \verb|\cref| command to cross-reference chapters, sections, figures, algorithms, listings, theorems, definitions, etc.
\item[$\square$] Make sure that your page numbers are aligned with the PDF's page numbers.
\item[$\square$] Try to avoid overfull or underfull paragraphs and pages.
\item[$\square$] Best practice: one sentence, one line in the source.
\item[$\square$] Borrow from other checklists.
\end{itemize}
\end{instructions}
